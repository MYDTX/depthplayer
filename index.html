DepthPlayer

2D → 3D media player for Meta Quest

What DepthPlayer does

DepthPlayer is a media player for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. It plays the videos and photos you already own and displays them in 3D by estimating depth for each frame on the headset itself — no PC, no uploads, and no account with us.

Features

  • Real-time 2D → 3D conversion, fully on-device
  • Local files — or stream from your own NAS (SMB / WebDAV / DLNA) or cloud drive (Google Drive / OneDrive)
  • Tune 3D depth to taste; toggle 3D on and off instantly
  • Subtitles and multiple audio tracks
  • No account with us, no ads, no tracking

How it works

  • Open a video or photo from your headset, your own media server, or a cloud drive you connect yourself.
  • The app estimates depth for each frame on-device and renders a stereoscopic view in real time.
  • Your files are never modified, never uploaded, and never shared.

What DepthPlayer is not

DepthPlayer does not create, synthesize, edit, or alter images or video. It does not swap faces, and it has no social, publishing, or sharing features. It only displays media that you already have, converting the existing frames for stereo viewing on your own headset.

Requirements

  • Meta Quest 3 or Quest 3S
  • No sign-in is required to view this page, and cloud drives are optional — they connect directly to the provider you choose.
